Privacy Policy

How Wayykup handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Wayykup is an alarm app that helps you wake up using challenges. This policy explains what information is used by the app, where it is stored, and when information may be shared with service providers. Wayykup does not require you to create an account to use the app.

In short: alarm settings and wake-up history are stored on your device. Camera images used for object recognition are analyzed on your device. If analytics or subscriptions are enabled in your app build, limited event or purchase-related data is handled by Mixpanel or RevenueCat and the relevant app store.

1. Information Wayykup uses

  • Alarm and challenge settings: alarm times, repeat days, sounds, challenge types and settings, selected Object Hunt targets, and (if you use that challenge) the contents of a linked QR code.
  • Wake-up history: alarm schedule and time-zone information, ring and completion times, challenge type, attempts, snoozes or bypasses, challenge results, and optional morning ratings. Some of this information is recorded in a local database on your device.
  • Onboarding responses: answers you choose to provide, which may include age range or age, gender, wake and bedtime habits, reasons for waking, alarm habits, and how you heard about Wayykup. An unfinished onboarding draft is saved locally so you can resume it.
  • Device permissions and capabilities: camera access for Object Hunt or QR challenges, notifications and alarm permissions, motion sensor readings for movement challenges, and audio access for alarm playback or sounds you select.
  • Device and app information: the app may use the device model, operating system, app version, and technical diagnostic information needed to check alarm compatibility or operate third-party SDKs.
  • Subscription information: if subscriptions are enabled, RevenueCat and Apple or Google process purchase and subscription status, product and entitlement information, and related transaction or diagnostic data. Wayykup does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

2. Camera, photos, and QR challenges

Camera access is optional and is used only when you choose a camera-based challenge. For Object Hunt, the app takes a photo when you request recognition and analyzes its image labels on-device. Wayykup does not send that photo to a Wayykup server for recognition. For a QR challenge, the camera reads the code and its contents are saved with the alarm on your device so the app can compare a later scan.

You can deny or revoke camera permission in your device settings. Camera-based challenges will not work without permission. Any temporary camera image handling or caching may also be subject to your operating system and device behavior.

3. Where information is stored

Alarm settings, onboarding drafts, app preferences, linked challenge data, and wake-up history are stored in app-private storage on your device. Custom alarm sounds you select are copied into app-private storage for playback. This information is not backed up to a Wayykup account because the app does not currently provide account-based cloud sync.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage will generally remove locally stored app data, subject to your device’s backup and operating-system behavior. You can also remove or change individual alarms and preferences inside the app.

4. Analytics

If a Mixpanel analytics token is configured for your build, Wayykup sends selected product-usage events to Mixpanel. Events can include alarm creation or changes, challenge type and alarm time, ring and completion timestamps, ring latency, device model, snooze count, subscription plan type, and successful object recognition. These events help us understand app reliability and feature use. The app does not intentionally send your camera image, QR contents, onboarding answers, or payment-card details in these analytics events.

Analytics are not initialized in builds without a configured Mixpanel token, and the app’s web preview does not initialize Mixpanel. Mixpanel processes data under its own privacy and retention practices. See Mixpanel’s Privacy Policy.

5. Subscriptions and service providers

When available, in-app subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat and the Apple App Store or Google Play. These providers handle purchase transactions, subscription status, renewals, restores, and related technical data under their own privacy policies and terms. Wayykup uses subscription status to determine whether premium features are available. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy, Apple’s Privacy Policy, and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Wayykup also uses Expo services to deliver app updates when configured. The app schedules local alarms and notifications on your device; it does not currently register a remote push-notification token for Wayykup push campaigns.

6. How information is used

  • To create, schedule, play, and manage alarms and wake-up challenges.
  • To keep alarm and challenge settings, preferences, and wake-up history available on your device.
  • To provide subscription access, process purchases or restores, and show subscription status.
  • To measure reliability and improve features when optional analytics are configured.
  • To diagnose technical problems, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

7. Sharing and international processing

Wayykup does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the providers described above when their services are enabled, or disclosed if required by law or needed to protect users, the app, or our legal rights. Those providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live and apply their own safeguards and policies.

8. Retention and security

Information stored only on your device remains there until you remove it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app, subject to device backups and operating-system behavior. Analytics and subscription-provider records are retained according to the provider’s policies and settings. We use app-private storage and rely on platform protections, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Use device settings to revoke camera, notification, or other permissions. Some app features may then be unavailable.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription through the app store where you purchased it.
  • Remove local information by deleting alarms, clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling Wayykup.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. Contact us using the details below to make a request. Information held by Mixpanel, RevenueCat, Apple, or Google may need to be requested from that provider.

10. Children’s privacy

Wayykup is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request. Where local law sets a higher age for digital consent, that local requirement applies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Wayykup’s features or data practices change. We will update the effective date above and, where required, provide additional notice or obtain consent.
