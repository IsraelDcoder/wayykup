Good questions, better mornings

Frequently asked.

What is Wayykup?

Wayykup is an alarm app designed to help you get out of bed by completing a wake-up challenge, instead of dismissing the alarm with a quick tap.

What kinds of wake-up challenges can I choose?

Challenge options include math, shaking, memory, and finding or recognizing an object. The available choices may vary by alarm and app version.

Can I set alarms to repeat?

Yes. Set an alarm for the time you need and choose the days it should repeat to fit your weekly routine.

Will my alarm work if my phone is silent or the app is closed?

Alarm behavior depends on your phone’s operating system, permissions, and power settings. Allow the permissions Wayykup requests, follow any in-app setup guidance, and test an alarm on your device before relying on it.

Where can I get Wayykup?

Use the App Store or Google Play buttons on this page to search for Wayykup. If it isn’t listed yet, please check back later. For help, email support@wayykup.app.

Where can I find pricing and subscription details?

Current pricing, trial eligibility, and subscription terms are shown in the app before you confirm a purchase.
