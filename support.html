How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about alarms, wake-up challenges, and your subscription.

Need a hand? Browse the answers below or contact our support team. When writing in, include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. Please don’t include passwords or payment-card details.

How can I make sure my alarm rings?

Keep your device charged, check that the alarm is enabled for the correct time and repeat days, and make sure Wayykup has the permissions requested by your device. Check your device’s alarm and notification volume, Focus or Do Not Disturb settings, and battery restrictions. Alarm behavior can vary by device and operating system, so test your setup before relying on an alarm. For an important wake-up, keep a backup alarm.

Why does a challenge need camera, microphone, or motion access?

Camera access is used for Object Hunt and Push-up Challenge. Microphone and speech-recognition access are used for Yell to Stop. Shake Your Phone uses motion sensor readings. If a permission is denied, the related challenge may not work. You can review or change app permissions in your device settings.

What should I do if a challenge isn’t recognizing my attempt?

Make sure the relevant permission is enabled and follow the on-screen instructions. For camera challenges, use good lighting, keep the target or your body clearly in frame, and try again. For Yell to Stop, speak the selected phrase clearly in a quiet space. If the challenge still doesn’t work, contact us with the challenge name and your device details.

How do I restore a purchase or subscription?

Open the in-app paywall and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account or Google Play account used for the original purchase. Store billing, cancellations, and refund requests are managed by the app store where you subscribed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store used to subscribe. On iPhone or iPad, open your Apple Account subscription settings. On Android, open Google Play’s Payments & subscriptions settings. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

What information should I include in a support request?

Tell us what you were trying to do, what you expected, what happened instead, and when it happened. Include your device model, operating system version, and Wayykup app version if available. A screenshot can help; please remove personal or sensitive information first.
